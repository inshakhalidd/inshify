The brief

Social media posts for tyr, a men's skincare brand: a product launch post, a product range post and a customer review post.

The idea

Soft olive and cream tones, natural textures and plenty of space keep the look clean and calm. Real product shots stay the hero, and the review post turns customer feedback into a scroll-stopping visual.
