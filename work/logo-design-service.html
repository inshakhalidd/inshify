The brief

Three logo directions presented as options. Concept work, not used by any client.

The idea

Three directions for one name, each built on a mountain peak: a layered purple triangle, a green twin-peak mark and a minimal blue line icon. Every option is shown on light and dark backgrounds, so a client can compare them side by side.
