The brief

Three brand identity directions for Deep Summit, each with a logo system, color palette, typography and mockups. Concept work, not used by any client.

The idea

One name, three moods. Violet stacks a layered peak beside a bold Space Grotesk wordmark; green pairs a twin-mountain icon with friendly lowercase Inter Tight; midnight blue folds the mountain into the word itself, so the peaks replace the double m in summit. Each direction is tested as an app icon, on a phone and on a business card.
