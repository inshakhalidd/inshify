The brief

Brand overview boards for Smile Brilliant, Razor and Kinfolk, designed to build their portfolio.

The idea

Each board introduces a brand the way a client would first meet it: logo, a short overview and real product imagery on one page. The layout adapts to each brand's character, from a clean clinical look for Smile Brilliant to bold red for Razor and warm earth tones for Kinfolk.
