E-Rising Trainers

( Paid client work )

The brief
Social media campaign, carousels and promotional posters for a training institute.
The idea
One red-and-white system runs through every post, so the whole campaign reads as one brand in the feed. Course titles lead in bold type, dates and seats sit in clear blocks, and every poster ends with a single call to action.

02

Brewkie

( Concept project · Brand identity )

The brief
Brand identity and guidelines for a coffee brand, from a concept brief.
The idea
A warm coffee-brown palette and a playful cup-and-cookie mark give Brewkie a cozy, friendly personality. The guidelines cover the logo, color palette, patterns, stationery and mockups, so the brand stays consistent wherever it appears.

03

Carousel Design Service: Valtora

( Concept project · Carousel service )

The brief
Six-slide carousel built to show the carousel design service. Concept work.
The idea
The carousel tells one story across six slides: a hook, a plain explanation, the numbers, a comparison, the common doubt, and a clear call to follow. A violet palette and 3D icons keep a complex topic easy to swipe through.
